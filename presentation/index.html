Industry Challenge

The Stray Animal & Rabies Crisis in India

2.76M

Annual Dog Bites (PMC12533994)

India accounts for 36% of global rabies fatalities. Over 2.76 million dog bites are recorded annually with heavy clusters in UP, MP, and Bihar.

40%

Unreported Incidents

Citizens lack a fast, frictionless reporting channel. Over 40% of injured street dogs never receive emergency medical attention.

0 ID

Zero Dog Traceability

Shelters suffer from duplicate rescues, lost medical records, and zero longitudinal tracking of Animal Birth Control (ABC) and rabies vaccinations.

Module 2

Automatic NGO Geospatial Routing Engine

📍

2dsphere Spatial Indexing

When a complaint is submitted, MongoDB geospatial queries calculate the exact spherical distance to all verified shelters in under 15ms.

🎯

Dynamic Coverage Zones

Shelters define operational zones (5 KM, 10 KM, 20 KM, 50 KM). The engine checks service capabilities (Emergency, ABC, Medical OPD) before assigning.

🚑

Leaflet Live Ambulance Routing

Interactive map draws direct Polyline routes between shelter HQ and the accident spot, displaying distance badges (`📍 4.3 KM away`) and turnaround ETA.

Module 4

AI Visual Canine Biometrics Microservice

1

YOLOv8 Detection & Crop

FastAPI pipeline detects canine faces and coats, normalizing orientation, lighting, and background noise.

2

512-Dim Feature Embeddings

PyTorch neural network extracts unique biometric feature vectors: coat coloration, facial patches, muzzle pigmentation, and ear notch side.

3

FAISS Vector Similarity

Ranks Top 5 candidate matches (e.g. DOG-0023 → 94% match) and presents side-by-side comparison to NGO officers to prevent duplicate rescues.
